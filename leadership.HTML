Senior Pastor

Pastor Robert Katweere

Pastor Robert Katweere is the Senior Pastor of Gospel Wonders Mission Church Bulenga. Since the church was founded in 2014, he has faithfully served God with a passion for preaching the Gospel, nurturing believers, and leading people into a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ.

His vision is to see lives transformed by the power of God, families restored, and communities impacted through prayer, worship, discipleship, and evangelism.
