Who We Are

Gospel Wonders Mission Church Bulenga is a Christ-centered ministry dedicated to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ, transforming lives, and raising faithful disciples. Since its establishment in 2014, the church has continued to grow spiritually while serving the community with love, faith, compassion, and the power of God's Word.


We believe that every person has a divine purpose, and through worship, prayer, teaching, and fellowship, we strive to help people discover God's plan for their lives. Our church welcomes everyone regardless of age, background, or nationality to experience the love and grace of Jesus Christ.

Our History

Gospel Wonders Mission Church Bulenga was founded in 2014 with a vision of spreading the Gospel, winning souls for Christ, and building a strong Christian community founded on biblical truth. What began as a small fellowship has grown into a vibrant church family where lives continue to be transformed through the power of God.

Our Pastor

The church is led by Pastor Robert Katweere, a devoted servant of God whose passion is teaching the Word of God, shepherding believers, and reaching communities with the message of salvation. Under his leadership, Gospel Wonders Mission Church Bulenga continues to inspire hope, faith, and spiritual growth through prayer, worship, and practical Christian living.

Our Beliefs

We believe in one true God, the Holy Trinity—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. We believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, who died and rose again for the salvation of mankind. We uphold the Holy Bible as the inspired Word of God and encourage every believer to live a life of holiness, prayer, worship, and service to God and humanity.
